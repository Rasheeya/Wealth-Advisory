Our Heritage

Built on Fiduciary Rigor & Enduring Principles

Founded in 2011 by veteran portfolio managers and private tax strategists, Stackly was created with a single mandate: to eliminate conflicts of interest in wealth advisory.

Traditional private banks often push proprietary investment products that benefit institutions rather than clients. We established Stackly as an independent fiduciary practice where every recommendation serves only your balance sheet and family vision.

Over the past 15 years, our team has steered families, business owners, and non-profit endowments through multiple economic transformations, safeguarding over $500 million in client capital.

"Our mission is to empower high-net-worth individuals and multi-generational families with clear, uncompromised wealth architecture that preserves capital, expands prosperity, and builds generational independence."
Marcus Vance • Chief Investment Officer
