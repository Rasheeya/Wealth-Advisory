Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our wealth advisory services.

What is the minimum asset size to work with Stackly?

Our Core Wealth strategies generally require a minimum of $500,000 in investable liquid assets. Our bespoke Private Wealth and multi-family office services are tailored for families and institutions with $5,000,000 to $100,000,000+ in managed assets.

Are you a fee-only fiduciary advisor?

Yes, 100%. We operate strictly as fee-only fiduciary advisors. We never accept broker commissions, sales kickbacks, or proprietary fund incentives. Our compensation is entirely aligned with growing and protecting your assets.

Where are my funds and assets held?

Client assets are held directly in your name at top-tier, institutional third-party custodians (such as Charles Schwab Institutional, Fidelity Institutional, or BNY Mellon Pershing). Stackly acts solely as your discretionary investment manager with limited trading authorization.

How often do we review our portfolio strategy?

While our algorithms monitor risk and tax harvesting opportunities continuously, clients receive detailed monthly digital reports, comprehensive quarterly formal reviews, and unlimited on-demand consultations with their dedicated Senior Wealth Strategist.

Can you coordinate with my existing CPAs and estate attorneys?

Absolutely. We frequently collaborate with our clients' existing trusted legal counsel, family CPAs, and business valuation partners to ensure comprehensive tax harmonization and estate synchronization across all jurisdictions.
