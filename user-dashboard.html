Advisory Guidelines FAQ

How quickly are capital withdrawals executed?

Liquid cash buffer distributions are transmitted via Fedwire on the same business day if submitted prior to 1:00 PM EST. Fixed income or fund redemptions require standard T+1 settlement.

Can Stackly synchronize with my family CPA?

Yes. We grant read-only CPA access to automated 1099 schedules and realized capital gain/loss ledgers to eliminate manual tax filing friction.

How are advisory fees calculated and billed?

As a 100% fee-only fiduciary, fees are calculated as a tiered basis point fee on average daily assets under management, billed quarterly in arrears. We never accept transaction commissions.
